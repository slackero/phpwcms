## Support phpwcms

If you or your company rely on phpwcms, consider supporting the project:

* **Contribute Code & Documentation**: Submit pull requests, bug fixes, or enhancements via [GitHub](https://github.com/slackero/phpwcms).
* **Community Help**: Answer questions and share solutions in the [phpwcms forum](https://forum.phpwcms.org).
* **Direct Sponsorship & Consulting**: For custom feature development, enterprise consulting, or direct sponsorship, contact [Oliver Georgi](https://www.phpwcms.org).
